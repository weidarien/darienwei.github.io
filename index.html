TUTORING RESOURCES COMING SOON

From the notebook

NOTE 001

The question before the experiment

Before choosing a technique, write the question in one sentence. What would count as evidence? What result would make you change your mind?

A well-framed question makes the next step smaller, clearer, and easier to test.

NOTE 002

Keep the unexpected observation

An observation that does not fit the pattern can be a measurement problem, an overlooked condition, or the beginning of another question.

Record it with its context before deciding what it means. A good notebook preserves uncertainty as well as conclusions.

NOTE 003

Make the process visible

Keep the reasoning alongside the result: what changed, why a method was chosen, and where its limits lie.

Clear records make it easier to revisit an idea, share it, and build on it.

THE RESEARCH TOOLKIT

Methods & protocols

Illustrative methods, ready to be replaced with your own.

01

Experimental design

A clear question, a testable hypothesis, and a plan that makes the result interpretable.

  • Define the outcome and variables before collecting data.
  • Use appropriate controls and document sources of variation.
  • Record decisions so another researcher can follow the experiment.
HYPOTHESES / CONTROLS / REPRODUCIBILITY
02

Observation & measurement

Careful measurement connects a research question to evidence. A method should make clear what is being observed and what it cannot capture.

  • Choose measurements that address the question.
  • Check calibration, consistency, and potential bias.
  • Keep observations alongside the conditions in which they were made.
OBSERVATION / QUALITY / DOCUMENTATION
03

Analysis & interpretation

Turn observations into an explanation while making uncertainty visible.

  • Inspect data quality before choosing an analysis.
  • State the assumptions behind a model or comparison.
  • Report uncertainty and distinguish association from causation.

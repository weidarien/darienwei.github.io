## Shared prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset: a high-quality organ close-up for Darien Wei's researcher portfolio, an illustrative editorial image rather than a diagnostic diagram. Create a single original richly detailed medical illustration with fine engraved ink contours, delicate crosshatching and soft hand-painted translucent color. Clean, elegant, non-gory, professional anatomical atlas style, dimensional forms, crisp detail. Square canvas, centered subject occupying about 80% of the canvas, generous transparent space around ALL edges. Genuinely transparent background. No text, letters, labels, arrows, numbers, decorative frame, watermark, or signature. No human figure. Anatomy should be plausible, with coherent connections, but this is an illustrative website artwork. 

## Subject prompts

### heart

A human heart opened in an anterior cutaway, clearly showing four internal chambers, septum, atrioventricular valves and fine chordae tendineae. Great vessels at top. Thick muscular ventricle wall in rich crimson, carmine and burgundy with muted blue-violet vessels; warm light on chamber interiors. An original cutaway composition inspired by traditional chamber illustrations, not a copy of any stock image. The interior structures should be the visual focus.

### brain

A three-quarter lateral human brain in lavender and violet, with one softly translucent area revealing branching neural pathways. Blend into a magnified network of a few exquisitely drawn neurons along the lower right, dendrites and axons connecting, luminous small warm-gold points at synapses suggesting signals firing. Keep the brain shape recognizable and the neuron detail coherent, elegant and uncluttered. Purple, indigo, pale lilac and small golden lights; not neon cyberpunk. The microscopic neural view is a conceptual magnification, not literal giant nerves inside the brain.

### lungs

A pair of human lungs with the trachea and branching bronchi, one lobe partially translucent to reveal the finely branching bronchioles. Add one integrated magnified cluster of delicate alveoli near the bottom right of the main lungs, without a frame or labels. Botanical-looking but anatomically plausible fine branching, airy and delicate. Sage, jade, mint and muted warm tissue colors, with subtle teal vascular accents. No flowers or leaves.

### liver

A detailed three-quarter human liver with the small gallbladder, a tasteful cutaway surface revealing branching portal vessels and internal lobular texture. A subtle integrated magnified hexagonal liver lobule at the lower right, fine cellular cords radiating from its central vessel. Earthy amber, burnt sienna, ochre and deep copper with modest teal-green vascular details. Clear visual hierarchy: main liver large, microscopic structural detail secondary.

### eye

A beautifully detailed human eyeball in side three-quarter cutaway, the front iris vivid teal-blue, cornea and lens translucent, inner retina layers and the optic nerve emerging behind. The intact front portion and sectioned back form one coherent globe. Fine vascular branches across the inner retina and layered structure of the wall. Ice blue, turquoise, navy and muted warm ivory tissue. Scientific editorial sophistication, not a disembodied realistic bloody eye.

### ear

A detailed anatomical cross-section of the human ear, showing the outer ear and canal on the left, tiny middle-ear ossicles, then the elegant spiral cochlea and curved semicircular canals on the right. One coherent connected anatomical composition rather than unrelated pieces. Warm golden ivory tissue, softly brushed bronze, dark teal nerves and muted blue-grey inner-ear details. Intricate ink drawing, quiet sculptural depth, calm and inviting; no head or face.
